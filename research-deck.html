The friction

What gets in the way

11 of 21 say they've sometimes struggled to find content that felt relevant. Open-ended answers cluster into four recurring complaints.

  • 11

    Content doesn't match personal style — the algorithm pushes one homogenous look on everyone.

  • 7

    Price and fit — items are too expensive, or don't represent real body types and sizes.

  • 7

    Repetitive content and too many ads or sponsored posts crowding the feed.

  • 5

    Can't find the actual item — no purchase link, or reverse image search fails.

"It can be hard to discover your own personal style and taste when you're being forced to fit into a specific one."

How interested, how often, and who they are

Interest level (1–5)

8/21 rated a 3, 8/21 rated a 1 — a genuinely mixed-involvement sample, not fashion superfans.

Search frequency (1–5)

9/21 sit at the lowest frequency — most discovery is passive, not actively searched for.

Age range

21–24: 11 · 25–34: 5 · 18–20: 4 · 45+: 1 — 20 of 21 respondents fall between 18–34.

Relationship with fashion

Research boundary: exploratory survey only, n = 21, no follow-up interviews. Short answers are directional signal — not equivalent to validated interview findings.
